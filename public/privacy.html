Course Radar

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Course Radar is a student course-planning tool for San José State University engineering programs, built as a CMPE 131 class project. It is not an official SJSU service. This page explains what data Course Radar uses and why.

Google sign-in

When you choose Continue with Google, Course Radar requests only the basic openid, email and profile scopes. From your Google account we receive and store:

  • Your name and email address, to identify your account.
  • Your Google account ID, to recognize you when you sign in again.

We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google data, and we never receive your Google password.

Planning data you create

When you are signed in, Course Radar saves the plans you make so they are available next time:

  • Your degree program, semester plan, completed courses and requirement choices.
  • Your weekly schedule and selected class sections.

Guest mode keeps a temporary draft without an account.

Assistant

If you use the built-in assistant, your messages and the plan details needed to answer them are sent to the AI and web-search services that generate replies. Do not include sensitive personal information in assistant messages.

Cookies

Course Radar uses a secure, HTTP-only session cookie to keep you signed in and a short-lived cookie to protect the Google sign-in step. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. Data is stored with our hosting and database providers only to run the service.

Deleting your data

You can sign out at any time. To delete your account and saved plans, email the contact below and we will remove them. You can also revoke Course Radar's access from your Google account permissions.
