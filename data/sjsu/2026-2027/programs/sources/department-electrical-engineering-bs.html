Advising

Your success is our department’s number one priority, which is why we want to give you all the resources you may need to do your best work. 

General Education Advising 

For general education advising, explore the Engineering Student Success Center to see all of the advising services available for Lower-Division Undergraduate students.

Major Advisor

Upper-Division students need to contact Professor Juzi Zhao & email address: juzi.zhao@sjsu.edu or Professor Robert Morelos-zaragoza & email address: robert.morelos-zaragoza@sjsu.edu for department approvals of all forms, petitions, etc. as well as other academic issues.

Undergraduate Advisor

Professor Robert Morelos-Zaragoza, Email address: robert.morelos-zaragoza@sjsu.edu or Professor Juzi Zhao & juzi.zhao@sjsu.edu

Upper-Division students need to contact Prof. Morelos-Zaragoza or Prof. Juzi Zhao for removal of major advising holds as well as advising in scheduling and choosing technical electives.

Major Advising Hold

Each semester, a Major Advising Hold is placed on student accounts to prevent them from registering for classes.  For Lower-Division students, see any advisor at the Engineering Student Success Center to remove this hold.  Upper-Division students need to contact Prof. Morelos-Zaragoza, Email Address: robert.morelos-zaragoza@sjsu.edu OR Prof. Juzi Zhao, Email Address: juzi.zhao@sjsu.edu OR make appointment for removal of major advising holds. 

Technical Elective Requirements

Students are required to take 12 units of upper-division EE technical electives. Students can take one three-unit upper-division course from other departments OR one three-unit EE graduate-level course as an elective with pre-approval from the Major Advisor (Prof. Juzi Zhao: juzi.zhao@sjsu.edu OR Prof. Robert Morelos-zaragoza: robert.morelos-zaragoza@sjsu.edu)

ENGR 100W

Students must complete ENGR 100W with a grade of “C” or better before enrolling in EE 198A. Note that ENGR 100W may not be waived based on Written Skills Test (WST) scores.
