Focus Areas and Electives

AE 110 - Space systems engineering
AE 119 - Finite Element Analysis for Aerospace Applications
AE 142 - Astrodynamics
AE 149 - Advanced Dynamics and Simulation
AE 166 - Rocketry
AE 169 - Computational Fluid Dynamics
AE 173 - Design of Uncrewed Aerial Vehicles
AE 180 - Individual Studies (project must be approved by AE Department Chair)
AE 199 - Special topics in aerospace engineering
Students with GPA 3.0 or higher may take an MSAE course as their elective

Design / Manufacturing

ME 110 – Manufacturing processes
ME 136 – Design for manufacturability
ME 154 – ME design
ME 165 – CAD in ME
ISE 114 – Safety engineering

Management / Economics

ISE 102 – Engineering economic systems
ISE 105 – System engineering & activity costing
ISE 151 – Managing engineering

Mathematics, Numerical Methods & Modelling

Math 112 – Vector calculus
Math 129 B – Linear algebra II
Math 133 B – Partial differential equations 
Math 143 M – Numerical analysis for scientific computing
ISE 130 – Engineering probability & statistics
ISE 170 – Operations research

Materials

MatE 131 – Fundamentals of additive manufacturing 
MatE 135 – Composite materials
MatE 160 – Fracture mechanics

Physics

ASTR 117A – Astrophysics I
ASTR 117B – Astrophysics II
Phys 105A&B – Advanced mechanics
Phys 120A – Laboratory electronics for scientists I
Phys 120C – Advanced physics lab: Optics
Phys 158 – Modern optics
Phys 160 – Thermodynamics & statistical physics

Structures

CE 160 – Structural mechanics I

Thermal/Fluids

ME 114 – Heat transfer
ME 149 – Acoustics

Controls / Mechatronics

ME 106 – Mechatronics
